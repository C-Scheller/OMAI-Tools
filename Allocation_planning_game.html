Network planning business game

Ten models, three plants. Decide which plant builds which model and how many cars it produces, so that total costs are as low as possible.

Each plant starts with its initial allocation, which cannot be removed, and a capacity of 500,000 hours. You can add further links between plants and models. Every additional link costs €1,000,000 and reduces the plant’s available capacity by 5 % (1 additional link: −5 %, 2 additional links: −10 %, …).

Each model has a demand of 10 thousand cars and needs a certain number of hours per car. Demand you do not fulfil costs €100 per car and needed hour, e.g. €1,000 for each missing Up. Production is planned in whole thousands of cars. A plan is feasible only if no plant uses more than its available capacity.
