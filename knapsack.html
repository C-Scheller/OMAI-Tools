The knapsack problem

You won the lottery. As a prize, you can choose as many items as you can carry. Which ones do you take?

There are plenty of items you can choose from. The portfolio of items is depicted as a set. The items are characterized by their mass and the utility they give you. Unfortunately, your backpack has a limited capacity of 60 kg.

You have to decide which items to take, and each item can be taken only once. The chosen items must not exceed the capacity of your backpack. You aim to maximize the utility provided by the chosen items.
